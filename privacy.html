Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Chargebees collects, uses, protects, stores, and manages your personal information while using the Chargebees website, EV charging platform, mobile applications, payment systems, and digital services available at chargebees.com.

Introduction

Your privacy is important to Chargebees. This Privacy Policy explains how Chargebees may collect, use, share, process, and protect information obtained through our EV charging services, charging infrastructure, mobile applications, website, EV driver portals, fleet management systems, and related digital platforms.

Your use of Chargebees charging services, website, mobile applications, EV driver portals, payment systems, and all associated services is subject to this Privacy Policy.

By accessing or using Chargebees services, you consent to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable laws.

Changes To Privacy Policy

Chargebees may update this Privacy Policy periodically to improve data protection, enhance security measures, comply with regulatory changes, or improve service transparency.

Updated privacy policy versions will always be published on chargebees.com.

Continued use of Chargebees services after updates shall be considered acceptance of the revised policy.

Information Collection & Usage

Chargebees may automatically collect information related to devices, charging sessions, payment activity, browser information, IP addresses, app activity, operating systems, and charging infrastructure usage.

Information regarding charging station usage, charging duration, charging power consumption, booking history, battery status, vehicle details, and charging session activity may also be collected.

Cookies, analytics systems, tracking technologies, and performance monitoring tools may be used to optimize customer experience and platform functionality.

User Information

User Information may include account details, contact information, booking records, payment details, EV charging activity, and customer communication records.

Chargebees retains User Information to process bookings, improve customer experience, enhance infrastructure operations, maintain security, and provide customer support.

How We Share Information

Chargebees may share limited information with payment processors, infrastructure partners, technology providers, legal authorities, and trusted service providers whenever necessary to provide services or comply with applicable laws.

Aggregated or anonymized information may also be used for analytics, research, operational improvement, and infrastructure optimization.

Data Security

Chargebees implements security controls, encryption systems, secure payment technologies, monitoring systems, and access restrictions to protect customer information.

Chargebees does not sell personal information to third parties without customer consent.

Data Retention

Customer information may be retained for operational, legal, taxation, security, and business purposes for as long as required under applicable regulations.

Accessing User Information

Users may update or modify account information through the Chargebees application or by contacting official support channels.

Acceptance Of Privacy Policy

By using Chargebees services, applications, charging infrastructure, website, or related digital platforms, you acknowledge and accept this Privacy Policy.
