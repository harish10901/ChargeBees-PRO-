FAQs

How do I enroll in ChargeClub?

Enrollment is automatic when you sign up on the Chargebees App. You start at the Bronze tier immediately.

What are the ChargeClub membership tiers?

There are five tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Red. Each tier offers increasing benefits based on your annual charging activity.

How can I earn ChargeCoins?

You earn coins for every unit charged, completing your profile, reaching monthly milestones, and upgrading your membership tier.

Do my ChargeCoins expire?

ChargeCoins are valid for 12 months from the date of earning. We'll notify you before any coins are set to expire.
