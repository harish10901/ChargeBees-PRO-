EPC Capability
Why Ground Mounted

Maximum Power,
Maximum Returns

Ground-mounted systems offer unmatched generation potential and flexibility compared to rooftop installations.

01
Optimal Panel Orientation
Panels tilted at the precise angle for maximum annual generation — no roof slope constraints. Install at the optimal 15–25° tilt for Indian latitudes.
02
Solar Tracker Systems
Single-axis and dual-axis trackers available — follow the sun throughout the day to generate up to 40% more energy than fixed-tilt systems.
03
Unlimited Scale
No rooftop area constraints — scale from 1MW to 500MW+ on any available land parcel. Modular design allows phased development.
04
Monetise Unused Land
Transform unused agricultural or barren land into a revenue-generating solar asset — long-term power sale agreements with guaranteed income.
05
Easier Maintenance
Ground-level access for cleaning, inspection, and repairs — significantly lower maintenance costs and faster fault resolution vs rooftop.
06
Carbon Credits & REC
Earn Renewable Energy Certificates (REC) and carbon credits from your solar farm — additional revenue stream on top of power generation.

Choose Your Tracker System

We offer fixed-tilt, single-axis, and dual-axis tracker systems — chosen based on your location, project economics, and energy targets.

Fixed Tilt System
Best for: Budget-optimised projects
  • Lowest capital cost per MW
  • Simple structure, minimal maintenance
  • Optimal fixed angle (15–25°)
  • Best for flat terrain projects
  • East-West racking option available

Best for: Maximum ROI projects
  • Follows sun East to West daily
  • 20–30% more energy vs fixed tilt
  • Proven technology, lower risk
  • Ideal for MW-scale projects
