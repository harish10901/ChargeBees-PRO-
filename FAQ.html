General

Chargebees is an EV ecosystem brand based in Hyderabad, building India's charging infrastructure through smart hardware, software and renewable energy. We connect drivers, fleets, station operators, and technology partners on one integrated platform.

We currently power 500+ charging stations across 100+ cities in India, with new locations being added every week. Use our Station Finder to check live availability near you.

You can reach us at db@chargebees.com, call +91 90000 40477, or message us on WhatsApp. You can also fill out the form on our Contact page and our team will respond within 24 hours.

EV Charging
