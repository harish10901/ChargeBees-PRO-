Why Go Solar at Home

Benefits That Matter To You

Real, tangible advantages for every Indian homeowner who makes the switch to rooftop solar.

Slash Your Electricity Bills
Reduce monthly electricity bills by 70–90% from day one. A typical 3kW system saves ₹2,500–₹4,500 every month for an average household.
Earn from Net Metering
Export surplus power back to the grid and earn credits on your electricity bill. Turn your rooftop into a mini power plant.
Government Subsidies
Eligible for central & state subsidies under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana — up to ₹78,000 subsidy on a 3kW system. We handle all paperwork.
Real-Time App Monitoring
Watch your system generate energy live via the ChargeBees app — track daily units, savings, CO₂ offset, and get instant fault alerts.
3–7 Day Installation
Our certified technicians complete most home installations in under a week — zero disruption, clean worksite, no mess left behind.
Increase Property Value
Homes with solar installations sell 4–6% higher than non-solar homes. Your investment pays dividends even when you sell your home.
