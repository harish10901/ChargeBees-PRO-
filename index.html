About ChargeBees

ChargeBees is an EV ecosystem brand focused on accelerating electric mobility through smart charging experiences and dependable infrastructure.

We connect drivers, fleets, station operators, and technology partners in one integrated platform to simplify EV charging access across urban and highway routes.

Helping you prepare for an electric future.

Chargebees® is India's fastest growing company to have a fully integrated technology, powered by software systems with hardware and renewable energy capabilities.

We have made a leap in our ecosystem and thereby created a playbook of Fleet Electrification (B2B) and Inter-City Retail EV Charging (B2C).

Our Vision: Energizing tomorrow, Today.

At Chargebees®, we envision a future where sustainable mobility is seamless and available to all. Our mission is to lead the way in cutting-edge charging solutions, spearheading innovation in electric vehicle infrastructure.

Through a focus on accessibility, community empowerment, and sustainable practices, we dedicate ourselves to creating a cleaner, greener future for everyone. Join us on the expedition to redefine electric mobility and fuel a sustainable tomorrow.

Our values

Empowering sustainable mobility, our values drive innovation, reliability, and accessibility in electric vehicle charging, fostering a cleaner, connected future.

We prioritize the safety of our users and infrastructure above all else. Our charging stations are built with multiple layers of protection to ensure a secure charging experience.

We are committed to building a sustainable future. Our solutions are designed to scale with the growing needs of the electric vehicle ecosystem.

Integrity is at the heart of everything we do. We believe in transparent business practices and delivering on our promises to our partners and customers.
